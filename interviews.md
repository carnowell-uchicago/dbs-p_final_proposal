# User Interviews: MPCS students during finals week

**Goal:** 3 or more interviews, in person or on a call, before Nov 3 at 5:30 PM.
**Rules:** Don't describe the game until the very end. Write down their exact words, not your summary. When an answer is interesting, ask "Tell me more about that" or "Why?"

## Questions

1. Tell me about the last time you were really stressed about a deadline or finals here. What did you do when you needed a break?
2. When you take a break from work, what do you actually do, and how long does it usually last before you go back?
3. Tell me about the last game you played during a study break. What was it, what device were you on, how long did you play, and what made you stop?
4. What's annoyed you most about the program or campus this quarter? *(This finds the jokes.)*
5. Tell me about the last time you compared a score, shared a clip, or competed with friends in a game. What was it?

*Optional, at the very end only:* Describe the game in one sentence and write down their first reaction word for word.
